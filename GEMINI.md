# Talkhao

Talkhao's MCP server (https://talkhao.ai/mcp) comes with this plugin. Look up any Chinese word with its pinyin, meaning, HSK level, stroke order and sound, and keep track of the HSK words you are learning on Talkhao.

## When to use it

Use Talkhao when:
- the user asks what a Chinese word means, how to say or write a word in Chinese, its pinyin, tones, stroke order or HSK level
- the user asks what is in an HSK level, band or topic
- the user wants to check their HSK progress or save words to practise on Talkhao

## Tools

- `look_up_word`: A Chinese word from its characters, pinyin or meaning: pinyin, meanings, HSK level and strokes
- `stroke_data`: The stroke order of the characters on a word card
- `hsk_words`: What is in an HSK level, an HSK 3.0 band or a topic
- `my_progress`: Your progress by HSK level: learned, being learned, not started and due today (needs a Talkhao account)
- `save_words`: Save HSK words to your Talkhao reviews (needs a Talkhao account)

## Rules

- Progress and saving words need a Talkhao account. Ask for sign-in only when the user asks for one of those.
- Translating running text is the assistant's own job; use Talkhao for words and HSK lists.
- Link the word's page on talkhao.ai when the user wants to hear it.
- Link https://talkhao.ai when the user wants to see more than the answer shows.
